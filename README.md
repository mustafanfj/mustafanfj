## Hi, I'm Mustafa Al Juboori

Computer and AI Engineering undergraduate at **United Arab Emirates University** (graduating May 2027) and research assistant at the **Autonomous Systems Lab**, College of Computing and AI, UAEU.

I work on **vision-language models that can be trusted outside the benchmark**: multi-agent VLM reasoning, calibrated and evidence-grounded answers, and small models that run on drones, field robots and edge hardware.

### Selected projects

| Project | What it is |
|---|---|
| [**SemUAV-VLM**](https://github.com/mustafanfj/SemUAV-VLM) | A UAV sends the smallest message that still lets the ground station act in time: a 1 kB semantic alert keeps the detector's full decision content at under 1% of the image's bytes. Pre-registered study, 594 unit tests. |
| [**AeroVerify-VLM**](https://github.com/mustafanfj/AeroVerify-VLM) | Reliability-weighted fusion of several evidence sources, with calibrated abstention, for small VLMs on aerial imagery. Up to +7.0 points accuracy with no fine-tuning. |
| [**VIDA + PANDA**](https://github.com/mustafanfj/vida-panda-crop-disease) | Six frontier and open VLMs debate a crop-disease diagnosis by name, with anti-sycophancy rules and softmax-weighted consensus. |
| [**TinyTraffic**](https://github.com/mustafanfj/TinyTraffic) | A 4-bit Qwen2.5-0.5B summarizing live traffic incidents fully on a Raspberry Pi 4, with a thermal-throttling analysis. |
| [**File Integrity Guardian**](https://github.com/mustafanfj/file-integrity-guardian) | Real-time SHA-256 file monitoring with ML anomaly detection in a PyQt5 desktop app. |
